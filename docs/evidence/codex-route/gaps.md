## Contradictions and unresolved alternatives

### C1. The round size and scope are undecided

- Lower value: **$750,000**, `notes/ask.md`, opening fundraising line, ledger `ask`.
- Alternative value: **$1,000,000**, the same line in `notes/ask.md`, ledger `ask_expanded`.
- The note links the choice to certification and a second mold set. Both ledger entries carry `status: conflicting` and reciprocal `conflicts_with` links. This is an unresolved choice within one file, not a disagreement between independent observations.
- Resolving question: first-round question 1. The answer needs a base and expanded scope with a costed milestone plan, not an average of the two asks.
- Conservative treatment: $750,000 for provisional calculations; neither option is confirmed.

### C2. Use of funds does not yet reconcile to either ask

`notes/ask.md` quantifies tooling **$85,000**, first-batch manufacturing **$96,000 plus shipping**, certification **$25,000**, personnel **$320,000**, content/creators **$60,000**, and launch marketing **$50,000**. These sum to **$636,000** (`draft_allocations_subtotal`). The same file proposes **$750,000 or $1,000,000**.

The differences are **$114,000** and **$364,000**, respectively. Shipping and buffer are explicitly unquantified; duties, second-mold scope, working-capital timing and other operating costs are not reconciled. This is an incomplete allocation, not evidence that the listed costs are wrong. The unallocated difference is not silently assigned to a buffer. The checker's use-of-funds warning belongs to first-round question 1.

No other numerical contradiction was found across the four files. In particular, the BOM line items sum to $96, 312 x $49 equals $15,288, and 312 / 2,140 rounds to 14.6%. The $96 BOM and $118 estimated landed cost have different stated scopes. The indicative 60.54% margin from $299 and $118 is consistent with the rounded approximately 60% statement. These arithmetic matches do not validate the underlying estimates or reported observations.
