# Examples

Everything here is fictional and safe to publish.

- `fixtures/meridian-ops/`, `fixtures/hearthbox/`, `fixtures/kestrel-health/`: three invented
  startups at seed, pre-seed and Series A, with files kept the way founders keep them
  (stale duplicates, a memo that rounds up, a confidential term sheet, a salary sheet).
  `fixtures/ANSWER-KEY.md` lists every planted trap and what a good run does with it.
- `sample-output/meridian-ops/`: a seed-stage folder after a quick run, the founder's answers
  and "build me the deck, PDF and web only, make it look great". Open `pitch/START-HERE.md`,
  then `pitch/deck/deck.html` or `deck.pdf`. Two amber "needs number" slots are left on
  purpose: the folder has no figure for them.
- `sample-output/kestrel-health/`: a Series A folder after the same steps, asking for an
  editable PowerPoint as well: `pitch/deck/deck.pdf`, `deck.html` and `deck.pptx`.
- `sample-output/hearthbox/`: a four-file pre-seed folder after a quick run, the founder's
  answers and "write the full narrative and the investor Q&A". See `pitch/START-HERE.md`,
  `pitch/narrative.md` and `pitch/investor-qa.md`.

Both samples were produced by the skill during testing (see `docs/TESTING.md`); the
`pitch/run-log.md` in each records what was read, asked and changed.

To try the skill on a fixture, copy the fixture somewhere, open your agent inside the copy
and say "Help me with my investor pitch. Everything is in this folder."
