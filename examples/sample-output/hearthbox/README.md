# Hearthbox

Hearthbox is the first countertop fermentation appliance that actually works for normal people. Think of it like
a sous-vide for fermentation: you put in the vegetables (or the milk, or the dough, or the tempeh beans), pick a
recipe on your phone, and the box holds the exact temperature, humidity and airflow for days or weeks. No
exploding jars, no mold roulette, no "is this supposed to smell like this". We have been running six hand-built
prototypes in Austin kitchens since March and people genuinely do not want to give them back. Kimchi, kombucha,
miso, hot sauce, yogurt, sourdough starter, natto, the works. Fermentation is a $XXB trend (gut health, home
cooking, TikTok) and the only hardware out there is either a $30 crock or a $4,000 lab incubator.

The box is one half. The other half is the recipe subscription: a living library of guided ferments from real
fermenters (we are talking to three pretty well known ones), with the box adjusting its program automatically.
Subscribers also get the community side (share a batch, get feedback) and we get the data: what people actually
ferment, how long, what fails. Over time Hearthbox becomes the platform for home fermentation the way a certain
coffee pod company became the platform for coffee, except good for you. We are raising a pre-seed to build the
production tooling and ship the first 1,000 units to the waitlist. Let's go.
