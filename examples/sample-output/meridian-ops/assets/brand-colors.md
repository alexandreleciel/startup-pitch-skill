# Meridian brand (v1, Jonas did this in an afternoon, feel free to improve)

## Colors

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `#0F4C5C` | deep teal, "harbour at dusk" |
| Accent | `#E36414` | signal orange, used for CTAs and the dot in the logo |
| Dark | `#1B1F23` | text |
| Light | `#F4F1EA` | background, slightly warm |
| Grey | `#8A9BA8` | secondary text, lines |

## Typography

- Headlines: **Space Grotesk** (Google Fonts). Fallback: Inter.
- Body: **Inter**.
- Numbers in tables: tabular figures please.

## Tone

- Plain. Operational. We talk like a dispatcher, not like a SaaS landing page.
- No "revolutionising logistics". Trucks arrive, trucks leave.
- German-English hybrid is fine internally, English only externally.

## Logo

`meridian-logo.svg`. A circle (the yard) cut by a horizontal line (the meridian / the dock edge), orange dot
for the truck. Do not stretch it.
