# Pitch notes (for the angel round) - June 2025

Rough notes for the 10-minute version.

**Problem**: dispatchers at mid-size logistics companies spend 3+ hours a day on the phone coordinating trucks.
Drivers wait 90 min on average. Nobody has data.

**Solution**: Meridian. Dock slot booking + driver comms. No app for drivers, just a link.

**Traction**:
- 9 paying customers
- EUR 12k MRR
- 0 churn so far
- Nordfracht pilot: 31% fewer dock wait minutes (3 months)

**Team**: Lena (ops, 9 yrs) + Jonas (eng, 11 yrs). 1 engineer joining in May.

**Ask**: EUR 300k angel round. 18 months runway at current burn (about 20k/month lol).

**Market**: ~14,000 logistics companies in DACH+Benelux with 2+ sites (desk research, Destatis + Eurostat
NACE 49.41 / 52.1 / 52.29). At EUR 15k ACV that's EUR 210M. Europe-wide maybe 5x.

**Competition**: whiteboards. WhatsApp. Then the big YMS tools nobody mid-size can afford.

**Why now**: driver shortage means carriers now charge for waiting time. The cost of the mess became visible.
