# Competitors and alternatives

_Updated 2026-07, Lena. Jonas please add the technical notes._

## The real competition

1. **Whiteboard + phone + WhatsApp.** 90% of our market. Free. Works until ~120 movements/day.
2. **Excel booking sheet shared via email.** Tried by most prospects. Dies within a month because carriers
   do not update it.

## Enterprise yard management systems (what prospects compare us to)

| Vendor | Positioning | Price (what we heard) | Why they lose mid-market |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transporeon (Trimble) | Time slot mgmt, big shipper network | EUR 3k to 5k / site / month + setup | Built for shippers, not forwarders. Heavy. |
| SAP Yard Logistics | Module for SAP shops | Six figures implementation | Needs SAP. Our customers do not have SAP. |
| Blue Yonder / Manhattan YMS | Part of the WMS suite | Enterprise pricing | Nobody mid-size buys a YMS standalone from them. |
| C3 Solutions / Descartes dock scheduling | Dock scheduling for DCs | ~USD 1.5k+ / site / month | North America focus, retail DC focus. |

We are typically 1/3 to 1/5 the price per site and live in 2 weeks instead of 6 months.

## Startups in or near our space

| Name | What they do | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cargoclix | Time slot booking marketplace, DE | Oldest in the space. Per-booking fee model that carriers hate. Mostly shipper-side. |
| Mercareon | Slot booking, retail DC focus | Owned by Transporeon. Retail-centric. |
| Two or three Polish/Czech slot-booking tools | Local, cheap | We see them in Polish-owned forwarders. Not multi-language, weak comms. |
| "Yardly" (seen at a trade show in Munich) | Looked like a prototype | Nothing live as of July. Watch. |

## Adjacent

- **TMS vendors** (several mid-market ones in DE) could add dock scheduling. They have not, because their
  customers do not ask the TMS team for it, they ask the site. Partnership opportunity more than threat.
- **Telematics** players know where trucks are but not where they should dock. Integration partners.

## Our angle in one line

Built for the forwarder's dispatcher and the subcontractor driver, not for the shipper's procurement team.
Live in 2 weeks. Multi-language. Carrier side needs no login.

## Honest weaknesses

- No TMS integrations yet. Every enterprise YMS has them.
- Small team. If Transporeon decided to go after the mid-market with a light product, it would hurt.
- We do not have a carrier network effect yet (32 customers is not a network).
