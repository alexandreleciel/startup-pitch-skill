# Founder memo, November 2026

_Lena. Written for the seed deck. Jonas has read it and disagrees with one paragraph (marked)._

## Where we are

We are at **ARR EUR 520k**, 32 customers, 7 people, and we have never spent a euro on paid marketing. Everything
so far came from Lena's network, two trade shows and customer referrals. We think that is the most important
fact in this memo: the market is pulling.

Burn is around EUR 95k a month all-in. We have about 7 months of runway. We are raising EUR 3M to get to
EUR 2.5M ARR and a repeatable sales motion in 24 months.

## Why now

Three things changed in the last three years and they are not changing back:

1. **Driver shortage made waiting time expensive.** Carriers used to eat the waiting time. Now they invoice it.
   Our customers pay EUR 20k to 60k a year per site in waiting fees. The mess has a price tag now.
2. **WhatsApp became the operating system of the yard, and it is breaking.** Every site has a WhatsApp group
   with 40 drivers. It worked at 100 movements a day. It does not work at 300. GDPR people are also starting
   to ask questions.
3. **Mid-size logistics finally has budget for software.** Margin pressure from the big players pushed the
   mid-market to professionalise. They bought TMS systems in 2020 to 2023. The yard is the next thing.

## What we believe that others do not

- **The driver side wins or loses the deal.** Everyone builds for the dispatcher. We build for a Romanian
  subcontractor driver with a cracked phone screen and 20 seconds. If he can use it, the dispatcher's life
  gets better automatically.
- **Mid-market is a real segment, not a stepping stone.** We are not going upmarket to compete with the
  big yard management vendors. There are ~14,000 companies in our size band in DACH and Benelux alone and
  nobody serves them.
- **Carrier data is the long game.** Once enough sites run on Meridian, we know which carriers are reliable
  across the whole network. No one has that today. (Jonas: "too early to put in the deck". Lena: "it's the
  vision slide, that's what it's for.")

## Who we are targeting

Head of Operations or Site Manager at freight forwarders, 3PLs and cross-dock operators with 2 to 15 sites
and 150 to 600 truck movements a day. DACH and Benelux first. We have learned the hard way that below 120
movements a day the pain is tolerable (Baltic Line churned, see interview notes).

## What the money does

- 60% go-to-market: 4 AEs, 1 SDR, 1 marketing lead. Move from founder-led to repeatable.
- 30% product: 3 engineers, 1 designer. Multi-site, integrations, carrier scorecards.
- 10% ops and buffer.
