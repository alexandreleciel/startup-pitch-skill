# Founders

## Lena Ostrowski, CEO

- 9 years in logistics operations. Last role: Head of Operations DACH at Transvia Logistik (mid-size forwarder,
  11 sites, ~900 people). Ran the exact job our customers have.
- Before that: 3 years at a freight procurement consultancy, mostly carrier tenders for industrial clients.
- Degree in Industrial Engineering, TU Dortmund.
- Why she started Meridian: "I spent four years buying yard management systems that never got rolled out. The
  problem was never the software. It was that nobody built it for a site manager who has 15 minutes a day for
  admin."
- Owns: sales, customers, hiring, fundraising. Does most demos herself (still).

## Jonas Hebbel, CTO

- 11 years as a software engineer, last 4 as engineering lead at a Berlin mobility scale-up (fleet routing team,
  ~20 engineers).
- Before that: backend at a telco messaging provider. This is where the SMS/WhatsApp carrier comms experience
  comes from.
- Computer Science, HPI Potsdam.
- Built the first version of Meridian alone in 5 months while Lena sold it to the first 3 customers off a Figma
  prototype.
- Owns: product, engineering, infrastructure, security questionnaires (sadly).

## How they met

Lena was a customer of Jonas's previous company. She complained about the API. He agreed.
