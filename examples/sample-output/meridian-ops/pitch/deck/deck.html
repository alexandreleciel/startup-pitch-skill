The problem

One Hamburg site paid EUR 38,000 in truck waiting fees in 2024

  • Three dispatchers, six phones, a paper notebook and a WhatsApp group of about 60 drivers
  • 60 percent of trucks arrive between 06:00 and 09:30; the docks stand idle in the afternoon
  • Carriers used to absorb the waiting time. Now they invoice it after 90 minutes

The solution

Carriers book by link, and dock wait fell from 94 to 65 minutes at one site

  • The booking link goes out with the transport order. No app, no login
  • Drivers get SMS or WhatsApp updates in German, English, Polish or Romanian
  • Dispatchers work from one inbox instead of six phones
