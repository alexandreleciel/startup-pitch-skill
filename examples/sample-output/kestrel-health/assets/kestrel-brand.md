# Kestrel Health brand notes

## Colours

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Kestrel Blue | `#1F3A5F` | primary, headlines, logo |
| Sky | `#DCE8F5` | backgrounds, cards |
| Warm Coral | `#E8735A` | accent, one per slide max |
| Ink | `#15202B` | body text |
| Mist | `#F7F9FB` | page background |
| Positive | `#2E8B6E` | charts: good things only |

## Type

- Headlines: **Fraunces** (serif, slightly warm). We are a health company, not a crypto exchange.
- Body and UI: **Inter**.
- Numbers: Inter with tabular figures.

## Tone of voice

- Calm, clinical, direct. We talk about people in pain, so no exclamation marks.
- "Employees" and "people", never "users" or "lives" in employer-facing copy.
- We say "physiotherapist", not "provider".
- Evidence first. If we quote an outcome, we quote the sample size next to it. (Callum insists. He is right.)

## Imagery

- Real clinics, real light. No stock photos of a woman holding her lower back in a grey office.
- Charts: one colour plus coral for the highlight. No rainbow.

## Logo

Wordmark "Kestrel Health" in Fraunces, with a small kestrel silhouette in the K's counter. Files on the shared
drive (ask Sofia). Do not use the 2024 version with the gradient.
