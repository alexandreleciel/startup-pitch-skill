# Objections we heard during the seed (2024), and where we are now

Kept this list from the seed process. Updating with status in Oct 2026 for the A.

| # | Objection (2024) | Who said it (type) | Status 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Marketplaces in healthcare have terrible take rates, how do you hold 20%?" | 3 funds | Holding 22%. Clinics accept it because we fill off-peak slots. |
| 2 | "Employers do not buy point solutions, they buy from their insurer or benefits platform." | 4 funds | Partly true. 60% of our employers came direct, 40% via benefits consultants. No insurer channel yet. |
| 3 | "Digital MSK (Hinge, Sword) will come to the UK and eat you." | 2 funds | They have not, meaningfully. UK buyers want in-person. We watch it. |
| 4 | "Physio supply is fragmented and you will spend all your money recruiting clinics." | 2 funds | Clinic acquisition cost is ~£340 per clinic. Supply has not been the constraint since Q1 2026 in the big cities. |
| 5 | "Why would a clinic not just go around you after the first referral?" | 1 fund | They could. They do not, because the employee comes via our booking, and the employer pays us. Leakage estimated under 3%. |
| 6 | "Your TAM slide is hand-wavy." (the £4.2B one) | 2 funds | Fair. Redone bottoms-up in docs/market-sizing-2026.md. Now £11B, but see Hannah's caveats. |
| 7 | "Regulatory: are you a healthcare provider?" | 1 fund | No. Clinics are the regulated providers (HCPC registered physios, CQC where applicable). We are a platform with a clinical governance layer. Legal opinion obtained 2025. |
| 8 | "Three founders, none has scaled a sales team." | 2 funds | Hired Grace (VP Sales) in Sept 2025. Pipeline is now 70% rep-generated. |
| 9 | "What is the outcome data?" | everyone | Brightmoor whitepaper (n=212). Controlled study planned 2027. Still our weakest point in my view. |
| 10 | "SMB churn will kill the cohort curves." | 1 angel (Imogen) | She was right. See founder-perspective.md. |

Seed closed June 2024: £2.4M, led by Thistle & Rowan Ventures, with 2 angels and a healthcare family office.
