# Kestrel Health. Board update Q3 2026

_Sent 2026-10-14 to the board and observers. Prepared by Priya (CEO) with Callum and Mateusz._

## Headline

Q3 was our strongest quarter. GMV of **£1.9M** (+31% QoQ), net revenue **£418k** at a blended **22% take rate**.
We ended the quarter with **41 active employer clients** (from 34) and **312 active clinics** (from 268).
**Net revenue retention 118%** on the trailing-12-month employer cohort. Headcount 24.

We are opening the Series A process in November. Target £9.5M (about $12M). See separate memo.

## What went well

- **Enterprise mix improving.** Two 5,000+ employee accounts went live (a UK supermarket's distribution arm and
  a facilities management group). Top 5 employers are now 58% of GMV. Great for scale, concentration risk noted.
- **Clinic supply is no longer the bottleneck** in London, Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds. Still thin in the
  South West and Scotland.
- **Utilisation** (covered employees who booked at least once in the quarter) hit 11.2%, up from 9.8%. Industry
  benchmark for MSK benefits is 4 to 7%. The Monday-morning Slack nudge experiment drove most of this.
- **Clinical outcomes whitepaper** published (docs/clinical-outcomes-whitepaper.md). Already used in 6 sales
  conversations.

## What did not go well

- **SMB churn.** We lost 3 employers under 200 employees in the quarter. Logo churn in the SMB segment is now
  running at ~22% annualised. Revenue impact is small (these are tiny accounts) but it is a pattern. Priya's
  view in notes/founder-perspective.md. Decision: we stop actively selling under 250 employees from Q4.
- **Payback crept to 9 months** on the blended employer CAC of £6,800 (data/unit-economics.xlsx). Enterprise
  cycles got longer (5 months average) and we hired two AEs in Q2 who were not yet productive.
- **Clinic payout timing.** Two clinics complained publicly about 30-day payout terms. We moved to 14 days
  in September. Costs us about £40k of working capital at current volume.

## Series A plan

- Target: £9.5M (~$12M) at a pre-money we are discussing with Ashgrove Capital (see legal/round-terms-draft.md).
- Use of funds: 45% go-to-market (enterprise sales + employer success), 30% product (outcomes tracking,
  clinician tooling, integrations with HR systems), 15% clinical team + supply ops, 10% buffer.
- Milestones for the A: £10M GMV run-rate by end of 2027, 120 employers, first non-UK market (Netherlands or
  Ireland) live.
