# Meridian

**Dock scheduling and carrier communication for mid-size logistics operators.**

Meridian is a B2B SaaS platform that replaces the phone calls, WhatsApp groups and Excel sheets that
mid-size freight forwarders and warehouse operators use to coordinate truck arrivals at their docks.

## What it does

- **Slot booking**: carriers book dock slots through a link (no login needed on their side). Dispatchers see
  a live board of the yard.
- **Carrier comms**: automatic SMS / WhatsApp / email updates to drivers when a slot moves, a dock closes
  or paperwork is missing. Replies land in one inbox instead of six phones.
- **Yard board**: tablet view for the gatehouse. Check-in, check-out, dwell time per truck.
- **Reporting**: wait time per carrier, dock utilisation, no-show rate. Exportable, used for carrier
  negotiations.

## Who buys it

Logistics companies with 2 to 15 sites and roughly 50 to 600 truck movements per day. Typically the
Head of Operations or a site manager. Not the Fortune 500 (they have SAP YL or Transporeon), not the
one-warehouse family business (they are fine with a whiteboard).
