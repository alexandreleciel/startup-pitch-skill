# Team

## Founders

**Priya Raghunathan, CEO & co-founder**
Previously 6 years at a UK health insurer, last role Head of Corporate Propositions (sold health benefits to
large employers, owned a £120M book). Before that, strategy consulting (healthcare practice). MSc Health
Economics, LSE. Started Kestrel after watching her own team wait 11 weeks for NHS physio while the insurer's
"fast-track" took 12 days. Owns: fundraising, enterprise sales, strategy.

**Callum Fairbairn, COO & co-founder**
Chartered physiotherapist, 12 years clinical, ran a 4-site private physio group in Manchester (sold it in 2022).
Knows the clinic side cold: how physios think about empty slots, why they distrust marketplaces, what a good
referral looks like. Owns: clinic network, clinical ops, outcomes programme.

**Mateusz Kowal, CTO & co-founder**
Previously staff engineer at a UK fintech (payments infrastructure, 2017 to 2023), then 1 year at a health-tech
startup that failed (he says the lesson was "do not build the EHR"). Built Kestrel's booking engine, clinic-side
app and the employer dashboard. Owns: product and engineering (9 people).
