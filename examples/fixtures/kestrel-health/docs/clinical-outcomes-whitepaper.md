# Clinical outcomes: Kestrel Health MSK pathway at Brightmoor Logistics

_Whitepaper, September 2026. Authors: Dr. Femi Adebayo (Clinical Lead, Kestrel Health), Callum Fairbairn (COO)._

## Summary

In a six-month programme with Brightmoor Logistics, a UK warehousing and distribution company, employees
with access to the Kestrel Health MSK pathway recorded **34% fewer MSK-related sick days** than in the same
six months of the previous year. Time to first physiotherapy appointment fell from an average of 19 days
(via the employer's previous occupational health route) to **2.3 days**.

## Setting

- **Employer**: Brightmoor Logistics, 2 distribution centres (Daventry and Warrington)
- **Participants**: **212 employees** who used the pathway at least once between January and June 2026
- **Total eligible workforce at the two sites**: 1,840
- **Comparison**: the same 212 employees' recorded MSK sick days, January to June 2025 (pre-programme), from
  Brightmoor HR records

## Results

| Measure | Jan-Jun 2025 (before) | Jan-Jun 2026 (with Kestrel) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| MSK-related sick days (212 participants) | 1,418 | 936 | **-34%** |
| Average days to first appointment | 19.0 | 2.3 | -88% |
| Sessions per case | n/a | 5.6 | |
| Patient-reported pain score (0-10), first vs last session | | 6.4 -> 2.9 | -55% |
| Would recommend (post-programme survey, n=161) | | 91% | |

## Interpretation

Faster access to physiotherapy appears to shorten absence episodes. The pain-score improvement is in line with
published outcomes for standard physiotherapy. The sick-day reduction is larger than we expected and larger than
most published figures for workplace MSK programmes (typically 10 to 25%).

## Limitations

- **Single employer, two sites, n=212.** Results may not generalise to other sectors or smaller employers.
- **No control group.** The comparison is before/after for the same individuals. Regression to the mean is
  likely: people who used the pathway in 2026 may have been having a particularly bad 2025.
- **Participants self-selected** into the pathway.
- Sick-day data is from employer HR records and depends on the employer's absence coding.
- Brightmoor also changed shift patterns in March 2026, which may have affected absence independently.

We consider this an encouraging pilot result and are planning a controlled evaluation across 4 employers in 2027
with an independent academic partner.
