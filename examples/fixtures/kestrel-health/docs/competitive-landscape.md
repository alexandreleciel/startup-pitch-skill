# Competitive landscape

_Callum, 2026-08. Battle-card version lives in Notion._

## How we see the map

Two axes: (1) digital-only vs in-person network, (2) sold to employers vs to insurers/individuals.
We are the only one sitting squarely on "in-person network + sold to employers" in the UK.

## Direct competitors (employer MSK)

| Company | Model | Where they win | Where we win |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hinge Health (US, public) | Digital MSK, sensors + coaching, sold via US health plans | Brand, outcomes data, scale | They have no UK clinic network and UK employers do not buy via health plans |
| Sword Health (US/EU) | Digital PT with sensors, employer sales | Strong tech, raised a lot | Digital-only; our customers tell us 40% of cases need hands-on |
| Vitality / Bupa / AXA PPP MSK pathways | Insurer-bundled physio | Already in the benefits stack | Slow access (10+ days), limited choice, no employer-level data |
| Ascenti (UK) | Owned clinic chain + corporate contracts | Owns supply, consistent quality | Owning clinics does not scale; 50-ish sites vs our 312 partner clinics |
| Physio-led startups in UK (2 or 3 small ones) | Marketplace, mostly B2C | Price | No employer product, no outcomes layer |

## Indirect

- **Occupational health providers** (big ones, often ex-NHS). Slow, expensive, mostly compliance driven. We
  partner with two of them rather than compete.
- **Employee assistance programmes** that bolt on "wellbeing". Not clinical.
- **The NHS**. Free, 14-week physio waiting lists in most areas. Our actual biggest "competitor" in the sense
  that employees default to waiting.

## Why we think we win

1. **Speed to treatment**: median 2.1 days from booking to first session vs 10+ via insurers and 14 weeks NHS.
2. **Hybrid**: digital triage and exercise programmes plus in-person when needed. Not ideological about either.
3. **Employer-level outcomes data** (sick days, return to work) that insurers do not give.
4. **Clinic economics**: we fill clinics' off-peak slots. They take a 22% cut gladly because the alternative is
   an empty 11am Tuesday.

## Honest risks

- A US digital MSK player buys a UK clinic network and does what we do with 10x the capital.
- Insurers get faster. Vitality has been talking about 48-hour access.
- Our clinic network is non-exclusive. Nothing stops a competitor signing the same clinics.
