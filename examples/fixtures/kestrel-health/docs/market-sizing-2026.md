# Market sizing (2026 refresh)

_Priya + Hannah (Head of Strategy), 2026-09. Replaces the seed deck slide._

## Summary

We now size the opportunity at **£11B** (UK + the five European markets we would enter next). This is a
**bottoms-up** estimate built from employees, MSK prevalence and spend per case. It replaces the **£4.2B** figure
from the 2024 seed deck, which was a top-down number (share of UK corporate health benefits spend) and only
covered the UK.

The two numbers are not directly comparable. We should pick one story and stick to it.

## Method (bottoms-up)

1. **Employees in companies with 250+ staff**
   - UK: 11.3M
   - Germany: 14.8M
   - France: 10.9M
   - Netherlands: 3.4M
   - Nordics (SE, DK, NO, FI): 5.1M
   - Ireland: 0.9M
   - Total: **46.4M**
2. **Annual MSK incidence requiring treatment**: 18% of working adults report an MSK problem causing work
   absence or treatment in a given year (public health survey data, UK and EU, we averaged). We use 18%.
   -> 8.35M treatable cases per year.
3. **Spend per case** through an employer-funded physiotherapy pathway: average 6.2 sessions x £65 = **£403**.
   (Our actual: 5.8 sessions x £62 = £360. We use the market average, not ours.)
4. **TAM** = 8.35M x £403 = **£3.4B in treatment GMV** per year
5. **Plus prevention / early-intervention programmes** (digital triage, ergonomic assessments, ongoing
   subscriptions): employers increasingly pay a per-employee-per-month fee. At £8 PEPM across 46.4M employees
   that is £4.5B.
6. **Plus occupational health referrals and return-to-work** case management: estimated £3.1B.

**Total TAM: £11.0B.** (3.4 + 4.5 + 3.1)

**SAM** (UK + NL + IE, employers 250+, physio-led pathway only): **£1.1B**

**SOM** (what we think we can reach by 2030): **£140M GMV**, roughly 13% of SAM.

## Comparison with the 2024 figure

| | 2024 seed deck | 2026 refresh |
|---|---|---|
| Figure | £4.2B | £11.0B |
| Method | Top-down: 14% of UK corporate health & wellbeing benefits spend (£30B) | Bottoms-up: employees x incidence x spend, plus prevention and OH layers |
| Geography | UK only | UK + DE, FR, NL, Nordics, IE |
| Includes prevention / PEPM | No | Yes (£4.5B of the £11B) |

## Caveats (Hannah)

- The PEPM prevention layer (£4.5B) is the softest number. It assumes employers will pay for prevention
  at scale. Today we earn almost nothing from it.
- The 18% incidence figure is "reported a problem", not "sought treatment". Actual treatment seeking is lower,
  maybe 60% of that.
- If you strip the soft layers out, the comparable bottoms-up UK-only number is about £0.9B, which is LOWER than
  the seed deck's £4.2B. Worth knowing before an investor does the maths in the room.
